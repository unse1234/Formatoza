# 60-Day Launch Plan

## Days 1–7 — foundation

Build:
- static site architecture;
- data-driven routes;
- design system;
- global metadata;
- sitemap/robots;
- core converter engine interface;
- first 10 image conversions;
- policy pages.

Deploy an actual working site as early as possible.

## Days 8–21 — launch cluster

Add:
- remaining image conversions;
- image/PDF conversions;
- CSV/JSON/XML/YAML conversions;
- subtitle conversions;
- first document/text conversions.

Target around 60 useful URLs.

Write/validate conversion-specific content rather than generating generic paragraphs.

## Days 22–30 — indexing and QA

Use Google Search Console:
- submit sitemap;
- request indexing selectively for important pages;
- inspect rendered HTML;
- monitor impressions;
- fix crawl/indexing problems.

Do not mass-submit thousands of URLs.

## Days 31–45 — data-led expansion

Find:
- pages with impressions but no clicks;
- queries where pages rank 8–30;
- conversions appearing in Search Console that are not yet dedicated pages.

Improve:
- title/description;
- intro copy;
- internal links;
- FAQ;
- comparison content;
- examples.

Only then add another 10–30 conversion pages.

## Days 46–60 — monetization/optimization

Once the site meets AdSense's requirements and is approved:
- add responsive ad slots;
- connect Google Privacy & Messaging/CMP flow as required;
- monitor page-level RPM;
- remove low-value ad placements;
- optimize pages producing impressions but poor engagement.

## Revenue expectation

The business goal is to have a chance at some initial earnings in two months, not a guaranteed amount.

The earliest meaningful leading indicators are:
- indexed pages;
- impressions;
- organic clicks;
- top-20 query count;
- successful conversions;
- AdSense approval;
- ad impressions and RPM.

Do not evaluate the business only by revenue during the first few weeks.
